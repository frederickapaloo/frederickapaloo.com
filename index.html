The Eden Within

What if Eden was never just a location, but a condition of the heart?

In The Eden Within, I take readers through three gardens: Eden, Gethsemane, and the garden of the heart. In each one, a person decides between their own desires and the will of God.

This book is for the believer struggling in secret, the young person overwhelmed by desires they cannot control, and anyone who hungers for deeper intimacy with God.

  • Three gardensEden, Gethsemane, and the heart
  • Old and new EdenWhat was lost, and what has been restored
  • Desires of the heartWhere desire begins, and where it leads
  • Stone and fleshWhy a heart hardens, and how it softens
  • Keeping your heartPractical ways to guard it every day

Called to guard the inner man
